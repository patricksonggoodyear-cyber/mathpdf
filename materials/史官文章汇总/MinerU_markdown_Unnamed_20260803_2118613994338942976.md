证明：对于任意正整数 n 以及任意实数 $x _ { 1 } , x _ { 2 } , \cdots , x _ { n }$ ，恒有

$$
\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} \min \{i, j \} x _ {i} x _ {j} \geqslant 0.
$$

解

令

$$
T _ {k} = x _ {k} + x _ {k + 1} + \dots + x _ {n}, \qquad k = 1, 2, \dots , n.
$$

展开各个尾和的平方，有

$$
\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} T _ {k} ^ {2} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \sum_ {i = k} ^ {n} \sum_ {j = k} ^ {n} x _ {i} x _ {j}.\tag{1}
$$

对于固定的 $i , j$ ，乘积 $x _ { i } x _ { j }$ 恰好出现在

$$
T _ {1} ^ {2}, T _ {2} ^ {2}, \dots , T _ {\min \{i, j \}} ^ {2}
$$

中，共出现 $\operatorname* { m i n } \{ i , j \}$ 次。因此，由 (1) 式可得恒等式

$$
\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} \min \{i, j \} x _ {i} x _ {j} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} T _ {k} ^ {2} \geqslant 0.
$$

原不等式得证。

因为 $a _ { 1 } \geqslant a _ { 2 } \geqslant \dots \geqslant a _ { n + 1 } = 0$ ，所以各个 $a _ { k }$ 均非负。令

$$
x _ {k} = \sqrt {a _ {k}} - \sqrt {a _ {k + 1}} \geqslant 0, \quad k = 1, 2, \dots , n.
$$

由 $a _ { n + 1 } = 0$ ，我们有

$$

因此

$$
\begin{array}{c} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} a _ {k} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} (x _ {k} + x _ {k + 1} + \dots + x _ {n}) ^ {2} \\ = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} k x _ {k} ^ {2} + 2 \sum_ {1 \leqslant i <   j \leqslant n} i x _ {i} x _ {j}. \end{array}\tag{2}

另一方面，原不等式右端就是

$$
\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \sqrt {k} \left(\sqrt {a _ {k}} - \sqrt {a _ {k + 1}}\right) = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \sqrt {k} x _ {k},
$$

故其平方为

$$
\left(\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \sqrt {k} x _ {k}\right) ^ {2} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} k x _ {k} ^ {2} + 2 \sum_ {1 \leqslant i <   j \leqslant n} \sqrt {i j} x _ {i} x _ {j}.\tag{3}

当 $i < j$ 时， $i \leqslant \sqrt { i j }$ ；又因为 $x _ { i } , x _ { j } \geqslant 0$ ，所以比较 (2)、(3) 两式可得

求最大的实数 C，使得对任意正整数 n 和任意实数 $x _ { 1 } , x _ { 2 } , \cdots , x _ { n }$ ，均有

$$
\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} (n - | i - j |) x _ {i} x _ {j} \geqslant C \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} x _ {k} ^ {2}
$$

解

首先把原不等式等价地整理成

$$
n \left(\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} x _ {k}\right) ^ {2} - 2 \sum_ {1 \leqslant i <   j \leqslant n} (j - i) x _ {i} x _ {j} \geqslant C \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} x _ {k} ^ {2}\tag{1}
$$

下面我们用熟知的手法来处理

$$
A = 2 \sum_ {1 \leqslant i <   j \leqslant n} j x _ {i} x _ {j} + \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} k x _ {k} ^ {2}, \quad B = 2 \sum_ {1 \leqslant i <   j \leqslant n} i x _ {i} x _ {j} + \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} k x _ {k} ^ {2}
$$

设 $S _ { k } = x _ { 1 } + x _ { 2 } + \cdot \cdot \cdot + x _ { k }$ ，同时 $T _ { k } = x _ { k } + x _ { k + 1 } + \cdot \cdot \cdot + x _ { n }$ ，则我们熟知

$$
\begin{array}{c} A = n S _ {n} ^ {2} - S _ {1} ^ {2} - S _ {2} ^ {2} - \dots - S _ {n - 1} ^ {2} \\ B = T _ {1} ^ {2} + T _ {2} ^ {2} + \dots + T _ {n} ^ {2} \end{array}
$$

从而

$$
2 \sum_ {1 \leqslant i <   j \leqslant n} (j - i) x _ {i} x _ {j} = A - B = n S _ {n} ^ {2} - T _ {n} ^ {2} - \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n - 1} (S _ {k} ^ {2} + T _ {k} ^ {2})
$$

所以 (1) 左端等于

$$
K := \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n - 1} (S _ {k} ^ {2} + T _ {k} ^ {2}) + T _ {n} ^ {2} = \frac {1}{2} S _ {1} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} T _ {n} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n - 1} (S _ {k} ^ {2} + S _ {k + 1} ^ {2}) + \frac {1}{2} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n - 1} (T _ {k} ^ {2} + T _ {k + 1} ^ {2})
$$

注意到，由 Lagrange 恒等式得 $2 ( u ^ { 2 } + v ^ { 2 } ) - ( u + v ) ^ { 2 } = ( u - v ) ^ { 2 }$ ，因此

$$
\begin{array}{c} K \geqslant \frac {1}{2} x _ {1} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} x _ {n} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n - 1} x _ {k + 1} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n - 1} x _ {n - k} ^ {2} \\ = \frac {1}{4} x _ {1} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} x _ {n} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} x _ {k} ^ {2} \geqslant \frac {1}{2} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} x _ {k} ^ {2} \end{array}

说明 $C = \frac { 1 } { 2 }$ 是符合要求的。

$$

$$

令 $n  + \infty$ 可知 $C \leqslant \frac { 1 } { 2 }$ ，故而 $C = \frac 1 2$ 是最优的常数。

设 $L _ { n }$ 为 $n \times n$ 矩阵，且

$$
[ L _ {n} ] _ {i j} = \frac {1 - (- 1) ^ {\min \{i , j \}}}{2}.
$$

解

取任意列向量 $\pmb { x } = ( x _ { 1 } , x _ { 2 } , \cdots , x _ { n } ) ^ { \top }$ ，作尾和换元

$$
T _ {k} = x _ {k} + x _ {k + 1} + \dots + x _ {n} (1 \leqslant k \leqslant n).
$$

$$
{\frac {1 - (- 1) ^ {m}}{2}} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {m} (- 1) ^ {k + 1},
$$

$$

$$

这正是这一换元的关键：原二次型化成了对角形。由于

$$
\boldsymbol {y} = L _ {n} \boldsymbol {x} = (y _ {1}, y _ {2}, \dots , y _ {n}) ^ {\mathsf {T}}, \quad y _ {0} = 0.
$$

将 (1) 式所对应的双线性型写出，或直接对相邻两行作差，便有

$$
y _ {i} - y _ {i - 1} = (- 1) ^ {i + 1} T _ {i} \qquad (1 \leqslant i \leqslant n),\tag{2}
$$

$$
T _ {i} = (- 1) ^ {i + 1} (y _ {i} - y _ {i - 1}).

\begin{array}{c} x _ {i} = (- 1) ^ {i + 1} (y _ {i} - y _ {i - 1}) - (- 1) ^ {i + 2} (y _ {i + 1} - y _ {i}) \\ = (- 1) ^ {i + 1} (y _ {i + 1} - y _ {i - 1}), \end{array}

$$

考察矩阵 A 所对应的二次型。由 $a _ { i j } = \operatorname* { m a x } \{ i , j \}$ ，可得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \left( \begin{array}{c c c c} x _ {1} & x _ {2} & \dots & x _ {n} \end{array} \right) A \left( \begin{array}{c} x _ {1} \\ x _ {2} \\ \vdots \\ x _ {n} \end{array} \right) = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} k x _ {k} ^ {2} + 2 \sum_ {1 \leqslant i <   j \leqslant n} j x _ {i} x _ {j} \\ = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} j x _ {j} \left(x _ {j} + 2 \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {j - 1} x _ {i}\right) = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} j (S _ {j} - S _ {j - 1}) (S _ {j} + S _ {j - 1}) \\ = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} j (S _ {j} ^ {2} - S _ {j - 1} ^ {2}) = n S _ {n} ^ {2} - \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n - 1} S _ {j} ^ {2}. \end{array}\tag{1}
$$

令

$$
L = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \end{array} \right), \qquad D = \operatorname{diag} (- 1, - 1, \dots , - 1, n).
$$

若记

$$
\boldsymbol {x} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c} x _ {1} & x _ {2} & \dots & x _ {n} \end{array} \right) ^ {\mathsf {T}},
$$

则

$$
L \boldsymbol {x} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c} S _ {1} & S _ {2} & \dots & S _ {n} \end{array} \right) ^ {\mathsf {T}}.
$$

因此，恒等式 (1) 可以写成

$$
\pmb {x} ^ {\mathsf {T}} A \pmb {x} = \pmb {x} ^ {\mathsf {T}} L ^ {\mathsf {T}} D L \pmb {x}.
$$

上式对任意 $\pmb { x } \in \mathbb { R } ^ { n }$ 均成立，而且 A 与 $L ^ { \mathsf { T } } D L$ 都是对称矩阵，故

$$
A = L ^ {\mathsf {T}} D L.
$$

由于 L 是主对角线元素全为 1 的下三角矩阵，所以 $\operatorname* { d e t } L = 1$ 。从而

$$
\det A = (\det L) ^ {2} \det D = (- 1) ^ {n - 1} n.
$$

故所求行列式为 det $A = ( - 1 ) ^ { n - 1 } n$ 0
